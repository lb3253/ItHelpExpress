Pricing questions

The things people ask before signing up

What counts as a "device"?

Anything with a screen or an operating system that your team relies on: desktops, laptops, servers, and work phones or tablets. Printers, routers, and switches are supported but don't count against your device limit — we're not going to nickel-and-dime you over a printer.

What happens if I go over my device count?

Nothing breaks and nothing gets cut off. We'll flag it on your monthly report and either add devices at $10/month each or move you to the next tier — whichever costs you less. We'll tell you which one that is.

Can I switch plans later?

Any time, up or down. Changes take effect on your next billing date and there's no fee either direction. Businesses grow, and sometimes they shrink — the plan should follow.

Are there setup fees?

No. Onboarding — installing monitoring, checking backups, patching what's out of date, documenting your setup — is included in your first month at every tier.

What if I need something that isn't on this page?

Ask. Bigger one-off projects (office moves, server migrations, network rebuilds) get quoted up front as a fixed price before any work starts. You'll never get a surprise invoice from us.
