FAQ

Straight answers

Is there really no contract?

Really. You pay month to month and you can stop any time. We'd rather keep your business by being worth the money than by holding you to a signature you regretted in month four. If you cancel, we hand over your documentation and account access — it's yours.

How fast do you actually respond?

Next business day on Starter, same day on Business, and within two hours on Growth. Those are the windows we commit to during business hours — in practice most remote issues get picked up a lot faster than that. If you need the two-hour window but not the rest of Growth, you can add it to any plan for $49/month.

What devices and software do you cover?

Windows PCs, Macs, laptops, servers, printers, network gear, and phones/tablets used for work. On the software side: Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, email, VPNs, browsers, and the everyday business apps your team lives in. If you run something unusual — industry-specific software, a point-of-sale system — tell us up front and we'll say honestly whether we can support it.

How does the AI part actually help me?

Two ways. First, on our side: AI triages incoming issues and watches monitoring data, so common problems get diagnosed the moment you report them instead of waiting in a queue. Second, on your side: we build custom automation workflows for the repetitive tasks eating your team's week — pulling data between systems, generating recurring reports, routing invoices. Those are $149 each, one time, and they keep working after that.

Do you only work with businesses in DFW?

On-site visits are DFW only — Dallas, Fort Worth, Plano, Arlington, Irving, and the surrounding suburbs. Remote support works anywhere, so if you're outside the metroplex the Starter plan may still be a good fit. Just know we can't drive out to you, and we'll tell you that before you sign up rather than after.

What's the cancellation policy?

Message us before your next billing date and that's it — no exit fee, no 30-day notice period, no phone call where we try to talk you out of it. Your current month finishes out and nothing renews. If you come back later, we'll pick up where we left off.
